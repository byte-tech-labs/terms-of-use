Your content and security Your Content belongs to You. The Company claims no ownership of it and receives no license to it. The Application stores Your Content on Your Device. We do not run servers that store Your Content, We have no account for You, and We cannot see, open or recover what is in Your vault. Content is protected with encryption keys that are derived from Your passcode and kept on Your Device. Your passcode is the key You are responsible for remembering Your passcode and keeping Your recovery email up to date. The Company has no way to open a vault for You. If You forget Your passcode and cannot use a recovery option, Your Content may be permanently lost. Recovery email If You add a recovery email, the Application sends one-time codes to that address to confirm it and to let You reset Your passcode. The address is used only to send these codes. Codes expire after a short time and must be entered in the Application. Updating from an earlier version When You update from an earlier version of the Application, Your existing albums, folders, items and notes are moved into the new encrypted store automatically after You unlock. Files are then encrypted in the background. Keep the Application open now and then and keep enough free storage until this finishes. Keep your own backups Devices can be lost, damaged or reset, and deleting the Application deletes everything in it. You are responsible for keeping backups of Content that matters to You, for example with iCloud Sync or an encrypted backup file. Our Privacy Policy explains what information the Application handles and how. iCloud Sync and backup iCloud Sync and encrypted backup are optional features of SafeBox Plus. When You turn on iCloud Sync, the Application seals Your Content on Your Device and stores only that encrypted data in the private iCloud database of Your own Apple Account. The Company cannot access it. • iCloud is provided by Apple under Apple’s own terms. iCloud storage, availability and any storage plan are between You and Apple. • Your passcodes are never synced. Each Device keeps its own passcode, and You enter it on each Device. • When something is deleted on another Device, the Application moves it to Trash on this Device rather than erasing it straight away. • Sync depends on Your network, Your iCloud account and Your available iCloud storage. It can be delayed or paused, for example while waiting for Wi-Fi. • If You stop syncing and choose to delete Your data from iCloud, that copy is removed and cannot be restored from iCloud. Security features Break-in alerts can take a photo with the front camera when someone enters a wrong passcode. A decoy vault opens a separate, harmless vault with a different passcode. These photos and the decoy vault stay on Your Device. You agree to use these features only to protect Your own Device and Content, and in line with the laws that apply to You, including laws on recording or photographing other people.
